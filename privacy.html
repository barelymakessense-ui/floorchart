Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

Disclaimer: This privacy policy is a basic template to get you started. Privacy laws vary widely by location. Please review it carefully to make sure it accurately describes how your bot and website operate.

At Floorchart, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information when using our Discord bot and website.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect the following data:

  • Discord Data: Your Discord User ID, username, avatar, and server (guild) IDs where the bot is installed. This is required to identify you and let you interact with the bot.
  • User Content: The flowchart layout structures, node descriptions, data points, or text configurations you actively create and save on our platform.
  • Technical Log Data: Basic internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, and timestamps when you visit our website, used solely for performance monitoring and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to operate and improve Floorchart:

  • To render, host, and display your saved flowcharts.
  • To verify your permissions (e.g., ensuring only server administrators can change bot settings).
  • To troubleshoot bugs, prevent abuse, and keep our infrastructure secure.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention & Security

We store your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Saved flowcharts and configuration data remain on our servers until you choose to delete them.

We implement basic commercial security measures to prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure of user data. However, please remember that no data transmission over the internet is completely bulletproof.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to wipe your information from our database, you can do so by:

  • Deleting your flowcharts directly through the website interface.
  • Kicking or removing the Floorchart bot from your server.
  • Contacting us directly through our support channels to request a manual purge of all data linked to your Discord account.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website and bot leverage components from third parties (such as Discord's Official API). These third-party services have their own independent privacy policies governing their platforms, which we encourage you to read.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Floorchart following any updates means you acknowledge and accept the updated policy.
